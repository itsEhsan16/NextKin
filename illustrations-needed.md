# Illustrations needed for the redesigned Figma boards

There are 35 illustrations to draw, listed below by page. Four more belong to archived screens and are not needed now; they are listed at the end.

Wherever a new illustration should go, the board shows a dashed lavender placeholder. Its frame is named `ILLUSTRATION IL-xx — <what to draw>` and is sized to the space the art should fill. To place your art, drop it into that frame or replace the frame, keeping the size and position.

Some sheet boards are drawn over a copy of their parent board, so the parent's placeholder appears there too. One drawing serves both: BILLING 02 shows IL-B1 behind its sheet.
